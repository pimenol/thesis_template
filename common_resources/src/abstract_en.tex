\section*{Abstract}
\vskip 0.5em

This bachelor’s thesis introduces a method for classifying Czech news articles by journalistic quality using \ac{NLP} and \ac{ML}. The multi-class classification system was trained on annotated data from Seznam.cz, a major Czech platform, where human editors labeled articles into four quality tiers: Insufficient, Short but Sufficient, Standard, and High-Quality. We evaluated several models, including a fine-tuned \ac{DeBERTaV3} transformer and a \ac{CatBoost} classifier with engineered features. Additionally, we developed a hybrid model that integrates \ac{DeBERTaV3} outputs with \ac{CatBoost}, leveraging both textual and metadata features. This hybrid approach achieved the best results, reaching a weighted F1 score of 0.78 on a held-out test set—comparable to human inter-annotator agreement. Our findings demonstrate that combining advanced language models with metadata can effectively predict article quality, offering a foundation for automated tools to support editorial workflows and content curation in Czech media.
