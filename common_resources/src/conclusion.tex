\chapter{Conclusion\label{chap:conclusion}}

In this thesis, we developed a robust classification system for Czech articles based on journalistic quality. We fine-tuned a \ac{DeBERTaV3} language model and combined it with a \ac{CatBoost} classifier using features such as text representations and metadata. The final model achieved a weighted F1 score of 0.78 on the test set. This performance is close to human editor agreement, showing that the system can reliably classify articles into four quality levels. The results confirm that incorporating linguistic and structural features improves prediction. In summary, our experiments showed that automatic classification of article quality is feasible and effective with the chosen methods.
